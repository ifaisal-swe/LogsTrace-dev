navigation:
  icon: i-lucide-radio

The overview, **Logs**, **Tables** and open tables update in real time over a WebSocket, with a polling fallback.

- **Logs** can be paused. New messages wait behind a **Show N new messages** button.
- Viewers receive masked messages on the live stream too.
- If your role changes or your session is revoked, the live stream follows within 30 seconds.
