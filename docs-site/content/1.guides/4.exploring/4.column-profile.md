title: Column profile
description: See what a column contains at a glance.
navigation:
  icon: i-lucide-chart-bar
---

Click a column header and choose **Profile** to see, for the current range and filters:

- Total values, empty values and distinct values.
- The 10 most common values. Click one to filter on it.
- For number columns (80% or more of values are numbers): min, max, average, median (p50), p95 and a histogram.

Use it to spot outliers before writing filters: an unexpected top value, a long tail in amounts, or a column that's mostly empty.
