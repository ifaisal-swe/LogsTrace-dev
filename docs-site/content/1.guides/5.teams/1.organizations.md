- An **organization** holds members and their roles. Invite people from **Members** in the organization menu.
- A **project** holds logs, API keys, tables, views and dashboards. Everyone in the organization can open every project, with the permissions of their role.

## Addresses

Organizations and projects get a URL address made from their name: `Checkout API` → `checkout-api` (then `-2`, `-3` if taken). Renaming keeps the address, so bookmarks, saved links and scripts don't break.

## Suggested layout

| Need | Use |
|---|---|
| Separate teams or customers | Organizations |
| Separate apps or environments | Projects (`checkout-production`, `checkout-staging`) |
| Areas within one app | [Channels](/guides/sending-logs/channels) |

## Names

Names can't contain links, domains, email addresses or hidden characters. This stops invites and shared dashboards from being used for phishing.
